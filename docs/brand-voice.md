## voice rules

1. **Talk like a person.** Contractions are fine. Sentence fragments are great.
2. **Get to the point.** Lead with the answer, then explain.
3. **Have opinions.** "We think X is better because Y" beats "X and Y both have tradeoffs."
4. **No corporate hedging.** "Some users may find" → "You will."
5. **Show, don't tell.** A code example beats a paragraph of description.
6. **Be funny if it's actually funny.** If it's not, just be clear.

## what we sound like

> hooked on facets is a filter plugin. It's fast, themable, and doesn't smell like 2017. We built it because the existing options are either pricey-and-old or pretty-and-locked. You should try it.
>
> Activate it on staging first. (This is true of every plugin.) HOF is designed to be additive — it doesn't replace WP queries until you tell it to.
>
> Because filters in WordPress are powered by hooks. Because the brand voice is "fun." Because the URL was available. Because *we are not naming our plugin Facetastic*.

## what we don't sound like

- ❌ "We are committed to empowering enterprise commerce teams with cutting-edge facet solutions."
- ❌ "Our innovative platform leverages best-in-class technologies to deliver unparalleled filtering experiences."
- ❌ "Frequently Asked Questions: Q: How might one go about installing the plugin? A: Installation may be accomplished through..."

If you wrote a sentence that could come out of any SaaS company's homepage, delete it.
